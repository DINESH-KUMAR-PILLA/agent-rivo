# Preparing and conducting a field visit

## Preparation
Before entering a store, confirm the establishment name and visit date. Review the latest authorised visit report if useful, but keep previous observations distinct from what is observed today. The previous report is context, not proof that an old problem still exists.

## Areas to consider
The visit may cover the front area, interior display, backroom, equipment and team organisation. These are prompts for observation, not mandatory findings. If an area was not discussed, the report should omit it or explicitly say it was not covered. Do not fill every section with assumptions.

## People and context
Record the names actually provided and the context in which they were mentioned. A staff contact is not automatically responsible for every problem at the site. A manager's absence is a fact if observed; it is not an instruction to hire, replace or evaluate that person.

## Ending the visit
Review the factual summary, positive points and issues before validating the report. Check numbers, dates, names and any follow-up that was explicitly requested. If something was said about the wrong store, remove it from this report before validation. A validated visit report records what the visitor approved; it does not certify that the observed issues have been resolved.
