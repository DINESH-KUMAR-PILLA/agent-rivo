# Reporting equipment and software issues

## Information to collect
Record the store, equipment or application name, an internal reference if known, the observed symptoms and when the issue started. Distinguish an observed symptom from a suspected cause. “The tablet does not charge” does not establish that its battery, charger or socket is defective.

## Relevant support contact
Software, shared tablets and connectivity questions normally go to the network's IT support team. Premises fixtures and signage hardware normally go to facilities support. If the category is unclear, the regional manager should ask the local contact which support channel applies. These are internal contact labels, not integrations provided by this exercise.

## How to write the report
Describe the equipment and symptoms factually. Include any requested follow-up exactly as the visitor intended. “Ask IT to inspect the tablet” is different from “the tablet was repaired”. Do not invent a diagnosis, safety rating, repair instruction or completion claim. If someone has already contacted support, say so only when it was reported.

## Information not covered
This document contains no replacement prices, warranties or guaranteed response times. If asked for one, say it is not specified here and that the regional manager should check the applicable support agreement. Do not supply a number from general knowledge.
